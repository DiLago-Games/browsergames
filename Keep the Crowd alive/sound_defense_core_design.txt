SOUND DEFENSE / FESTIVAL DEFENSE - CORE GAME DESIGN

==================================================
CORE FANTASY
==================================================

The player is not just defending a base.
The player is:
- a DJ
- a performer
- a festival architect
- a crowd controller

The goal is to keep the concert alive, attract more people, and maintain hype while surviving enemy waves.

The battlefield becomes a living music festival.

==================================================
HIGH CONCEPT
==================================================

A round-based Tower Defense game where:
- towers create music loops
- music affects gameplay
- tower combinations create resonances
- the crowd reacts to the quality of the performance
- enemies try to destroy the atmosphere of the show

The music itself becomes:
- gameplay
- visual feedback
- progression
- emotional reinforcement

==================================================
MAIN GAMEPLAY LOOP
==================================================

1. Build towers
2. Towers generate music stems
3. Music creates synergies and gameplay effects
4. Crowd reacts to the performance
5. Hype increases or decreases
6. Enemy waves attack the festival
7. Player adapts the soundscape
8. Festival escalates visually and musically

Over time:
- more people arrive
- visuals intensify
- music becomes fuller
- enemies become more aggressive
- the stage evolves

==================================================
THE CROWD SYSTEM
==================================================

The crowd is effectively the player's HP bar.

GOOD PERFORMANCE:
- more people arrive
- people dance
- hype rises
- stage upgrades unlock
- effects intensify
- rewards increase

BAD PERFORMANCE:
- people leave
- atmosphere collapses
- panic spreads
- visuals weaken
- income drops
- eventual defeat

Crowd reactions are immediate visual feedback.

==================================================
THE HYPE SYSTEM
==================================================

Hype is the core resource.

Hype increases through:
- harmonic tower combinations
- combo chains
- synchronized beats
- successful defense
- beat drops
- long resonance streaks

Hype decreases through:
- enemies reaching the crowd
- audio corruption
- broken harmonies
- repetitive gameplay
- destroyed towers

High hype unlocks:
- special abilities
- stage transformations
- crowd bonuses
- stronger resonance effects

==================================================
TOWER CATEGORIES
==================================================

1. BASS TOWERS
- crowd control
- knockback
- heavy AoE
- deep bass/kick sounds

2. PERCUSSION TOWERS
- fast attacks
- combo setup
- rhythm stacks
- snare/hat/glitch sounds

3. MELODY TOWERS
- high damage
- precision attacks
- beams/chains
- synth leads/arpeggios

4. AMBIENT TOWERS
- buffs/debuffs
- aura control
- healing/support
- pads/atmospheres

5. NOISE TOWERS
- chaos mechanics
- distortion
- risky random effects
- glitch/static audio

==================================================
THE RESONANCE SYSTEM
==================================================

The resonance system is the game's main innovation.

Towers have:
- BPM
- rhythm patterns
- sound categories
- tonal identities

When compatible towers interact:
- special gameplay effects activate
- music evolves dynamically
- visuals intensify

Examples:
- synchronized BPM -> faster attack speed
- harmonic combinations -> chain explosions
- layered percussion -> overdrive mode
- bass + melody sync -> amplified lasers

The player is effectively composing combat systems through sound.

==================================================
AUDIO DESIGN PRINCIPLES
==================================================

IMPORTANT:
The game must NEVER feel like music production software.

The player should:
- feel powerful
- feel creative
- sound good automatically

The game assists harmony behind the scenes.

==================================================
GUIDED HARMONY SYSTEM
==================================================

The game automatically prevents:
- terrible sounding combinations
- extreme dissonance
- rhythm collapse

The game subtly guides:
- compatible BPMs
- harmonic layering
- smooth transitions

Result:
Every player can create satisfying music.

==================================================
ADAPTIVE MUSIC SYSTEM
==================================================

Every tower adds a separate music layer ("stem").

Examples:
- bass layer
- drum layer
- melody layer
- ambient layer
- FX layer

As the match progresses:
- the soundtrack grows
- intensity rises
- crowd energy increases

The soundtrack is generated dynamically from gameplay.

==================================================
QUANTIZATION SYSTEM
==================================================

All audio actions snap to rhythm timing.

Example:
If the player places a tower:
- it waits for the next beat
- then joins the music perfectly

Result:
The entire game feels synchronized and professional.

==================================================
ENEMY DESIGN
==================================================

Enemies attack:
- towers
- crowd morale
- musical harmony

Enemy examples:

1. NOISE PARASITES
- corrupt audio zones

2. MOOD KILLERS
- reduce crowd hype

3. RIOT UNITS
- create panic in the audience

4. SILENCE FIELDS
- disable music areas

5. COPYCATS
- steal tower rhythms

==================================================
VISUAL DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
==================================================

The visuals react heavily to music.

Examples:
- lights pulse with BPM
- lasers synchronize
- crowd jumps during drops
- stage transforms with hype
- environment reacts to resonance

The battlefield should feel alive.
