KEEP THE CROWD ALIVE - MUSIC & AUDIO DESIGN PLAN FOR HTML5 / VS CODE

==================================================
PROJECT CONTEXT
==================================================

Game Title:
Keep the Crowd Alive

Platform:
HTML5 Browser Game

Development Environment:
Visual Studio Code

Suggested Tech Stack:
- JavaScript or TypeScript
- Phaser 3
- Web Audio API
- Howler.js
- Vite

Core Fantasy:
The player builds a living festival soundtrack through tower placement.
The music is not only background atmosphere.
The music is gameplay feedback, crowd control, hype generation, and emotional escalation.

==================================================
MAIN AUDIO VISION
==================================================

The game should sound like:

"An illegal cyberpunk rave at the end of the world."

The music should feel:
- powerful
- physical
- futuristic
- dark
- euphoric
- energetic
- slightly dangerous

The player should immediately feel:
- bass pressure
- festival energy
- crowd hype
- tension
- escalation

The music must pull people into the game within seconds.

==================================================
CORE MUSIC DIRECTION
==================================================

The soundtrack should combine elements from:

1. Bass House
Purpose:
- groove
- accessibility
- strong dance energy

2. Drum & Bass
Purpose:
- speed
- intensity
- movement
- chaos

3. Industrial Techno
Purpose:
- pressure
- darkness
- dystopian atmosphere

4. Synthwave
Purpose:
- neon identity
- emotion
- futuristic atmosphere

5. Dubstep / Bass Drops
Purpose:
- peak moments
- impact
- dramatic combat bursts

6. Glitch / IDM Elements
Purpose:
- enemy corruption
- audio disruption
- noise mechanics

==================================================
WHAT THE MUSIC SHOULD NOT BE
==================================================

Avoid:
- generic stock EDM
- cheesy happy dance music
- licensed pop-style tracks
- orchestral fantasy music
- chiptune as main identity
- overly complex melodies
- music that sounds like a DAW tutorial
- loops that become annoying quickly

The music should prioritize:
- rhythm
- bass
- groove
- contrast
- impact
- escalation

==================================================
IMPORTANT PRINCIPLE
==================================================

The game should NOT use finished static songs as the main system.

Instead:
The game should use modular dynamic music layers.

Every tower, wave, crowd state, and enemy state can add, remove, filter, or corrupt music layers.

The run itself becomes the song.

==================================================
MODULAR MUSIC STRUCTURE
==================================================

Each music track should be split into stems.

Recommended stems:

1. KICK
Main pulse of the track.

2. SUB BASS
Deep low-end energy.

3. BASSLINE
Main groove and movement.

4. PERCUSSION
Snares, claps, hats, toms, glitch hits.

5. SYNTH ARP
Rhythmic melodic motion.

6. LEAD SYNTH
Strong melodic identity or combat energy.

7. PAD / ATMOSPHERE
Emotional background layer.

8. FX
Risers, impacts, sweeps, transitions.

9. CROWD
Cheers, chants, stomps, claps.

10. CORRUPTION
Noise, glitches, distortion, broken rhythms.

==================================================
SUGGESTED BPM RANGES
==================================================

DEFAULT COMBAT BPM:
120 - 140 BPM

Use for:
- normal waves
- readable gameplay
- festival groove
- early and mid-game

HIGH HYPE BPM:
150 - 170 BPM

Use for:
- late-game pressure
- intense waves
- boss phases
- panic states
- high-energy drops

TENSION BPM:
90 - 110 BPM

Use for:
- boss intros
- near-loss states
- build-up sections
- dramatic silence before drops

MVP Recommendation:
Start with 128 BPM.

Why:
- classic dance tempo
- easy to loop
- works well for house, techno, synthwave, and tower timing

==================================================
AUDIO STATES
==================================================

The audio system should support several global states.

==================================================
1. IDLE / PRE-WAVE
==================================================

Purpose:
The show is preparing.

Music:
- low ambience
- crowd murmur
- distant sub pulse
- light synth pad

Visual Feeling:
- lights warming up
- crowd waiting
- stage breathing

==================================================
2. ACTIVE WAVE
==================================================

Purpose:
Core gameplay.

Music:
- kick starts
- bassline active
- percussion layers enter
- towers add stems

Visual Feeling:
- stage active
- crowd dancing
- towers pulsing

==================================================
3. HIGH HYPE
==================================================

Purpose:
Player is doing well.

Music:
- more percussion
- bigger bass
- crowd chants
- stronger synths
- brighter mix

Visual Feeling:
- crowd jumps
- stage expands
- lights intensify

==================================================
4. PANIC / LOW HYPE
==================================================

Purpose:
Player is close to losing control.

Music:
- layers drop out
- low-pass filtering
- unstable rhythm
- crowd sounds become nervous
- corruption elements increase

Visual Feeling:
- lights flicker
- crowd splits
- enemies feel more dangerous

==================================================
5. BUILD-UP
==================================================

Purpose:
Prepare a drop or major resonance moment.

Music:
- kick may reduce
- riser starts
- snare build
- crowd chant grows
- synth tension increases

Visual Feeling:
- lights dim
- camera tightens
- crowd anticipation

==================================================
6. DROP
==================================================

Purpose:
Massive release moment.

Music:
- full bass
- strong kick
- heavy synths
- crowd explosion
- impact FX

Gameplay:
- enemies can be pushed back
- hype spikes
- resonance effects trigger
- towers overdrive briefly

Visual Feeling:
- full-screen pulse
- lasers fire
- crowd jumps together
- particles explode

==================================================
7. BOSS CORRUPTION
==================================================

Purpose:
Boss attacks the music system.

Music:
- stems become distorted
- beat desync effects
- corrupted bass
- glitch interruptions
- silence moments

Visual Feeling:
- arena becomes unstable
- stage flickers
- crowd panics

==================================================
AUDIO AS GAMEPLAY FEEDBACK
==================================================

The player should hear whether they are doing well.

Examples:

GOOD BUILD:
- fuller mix
- stronger bass
- crowd cheers louder
- clean resonance sounds
- harmonized tower layers

BAD BUILD:
- thin mix
- weak beat
- muted crowd
- broken rhythm
- distortion increases

HIGH HYPE:
- crowd chants
- brighter synths
- more layers
- stronger drops

LOW HYPE:
- music loses energy
- crowd fades out
- percussion becomes sparse
- low-pass filters activate

ENEMY CORRUPTION:
- static
- bitcrush
- tape-stop effects
- random glitch hits
- silence pockets

==================================================
TOWER AUDIO DESIGN
==================================================

Each tower type should contribute musically and mechanically.

==================================================
1. BASS TOWER
==================================================

Gameplay Role:
- slow
- knockback
- area control
- heavy impact

Audio Identity:
- kick
- sub bass
- low synth pulse
- impact booms

Visual Sync:
- large pulse rings
- screen shake
- floor shockwaves

Implementation Idea:
Bass tower attacks should trigger on strong beats.

==================================================
2. PERCUSSION TOWER
==================================================

Gameplay Role:
- fast attacks
- marking enemies
- combo setup
- rhythm control

Audio Identity:
- hi-hats
- claps
- snares
- glitch clicks
- metallic hits

Visual Sync:
- small sharp flashes
- rhythmic strobe
- beat markers

Implementation Idea:
Percussion tower attacks can trigger on 8th or 16th notes.

==================================================
3. MELODY TOWER
==================================================

Gameplay Role:
- high damage
- chain attacks
- single-target burst
- beam effects

Audio Identity:
- arpeggios
- synth leads
- plucks
- melodic laser tones

Visual Sync:
- laser beams
- color trails
- waveform lines

Implementation Idea:
Melody towers should add musical identity to the player's build.

==================================================
4. AMBIENT TOWER
==================================================

Gameplay Role:
- buffs
- morale protection
- crowd stabilization
- aura effects

Audio Identity:
- pads
- atmospheric textures
- reverb swells
- soft chords

Visual Sync:
- glowing aura rings
- smooth light waves
- calm crowd effects

Implementation Idea:
Ambient towers should make the mix feel wider and more stable.

==================================================
5. NOISE TOWER
==================================================

Gameplay Role:
- corruption control
- chaos effects
- risky damage
- enemy disruption

Audio Identity:
- distortion
- static
- glitch
- bitcrush
- broken rhythms

Visual Sync:
- glitch shader
- fragmented particles
- screen tearing effects

Implementation Idea:
Noise towers should be powerful but dangerous if overused.

==================================================
RESONANCE AUDIO SYSTEM
==================================================

Resonance happens when compatible towers work together.

Examples:

BASS + PERCUSSION:
Creates a stronger groove.
Effects:
- higher attack speed
- stronger crowd dancing
- bigger kick impact

BASS + MELODY:
Creates a powerful drop line.
Effects:
- laser amplification
- area burst
- hype spike

PERCUSSION + MELODY:
Creates rhythmic precision.
Effects:
- crit chance
- chain hits
- marked enemies take more damage

AMBIENT + ANY:
Stabilizes and improves musical clarity.
Effects:
- longer resonance duration
- slower hype decay
- panic resistance

NOISE + MELODY:
Creates aggressive glitch lead.
Effects:
- corruption damage
- armor break
- risk of crowd discomfort

==================================================
QUANTIZATION RULES
==================================================

All major audio events should snap to the beat grid.

Examples:
- tower loop starts on next bar
- drop triggers on bar start
- resonance activates on beat
- new stem fades in at phrase boundary

Recommended timing:
- 1 beat for small attacks
- 1 bar for tower loop activation
- 4 bars for major transitions
- 8 or 16 bars for drops and boss sections

Why:
This keeps the game sounding musical even when the player acts randomly.

==================================================
FIRST MVP MUSIC SYSTEM
==================================================

For the first playable prototype, keep it simple.

Use:
- one BPM: 128 BPM
- one key: A minor or F minor
- one main loop set
- 3 tower audio layers
- 1 crowd layer
- 1 corruption layer
- 1 drop event

MVP Layers:
1. base ambience
2. kick loop
3. bass loop
4. percussion loop
5. synth arp loop
6. crowd cheer loop
7. corruption noise loop
8. drop impact

MVP Tower Audio:
- Bass Tower adds bass loop
- Percussion Tower adds percussion loop
- Melody Tower adds synth arp loop

MVP Crowd Audio:
- crowd volume increases with hype
- crowd volume decreases with panic

MVP Enemy Audio:
- Noise Parasite increases corruption layer
- Silence Drone mutes tower layers nearby
- Mood Killer lowers crowd layer

==================================================
IMPLEMENTATION PRIORITY
==================================================

PHASE 1:
Create basic loop playback.

PHASE 2:
Add global BPM clock.

PHASE 3:
Add quantized tower layer activation.

PHASE 4:
Connect hype value to crowd volume.

PHASE 5:
Connect enemy corruption to distortion/noise layer.

PHASE 6:
Add build-up and drop event.

PHASE 7:
Add resonance-specific sound effects.

PHASE 8:
Add filters:
- low-pass for low hype
- distortion for corruption
- volume ducking for drop impact

==================================================
DROP DESIGN
==================================================

A drop should be a major reward moment.

Trigger Conditions:
- high resonance
- full hype bar
- boss phase transition
- player ultimate ability

Drop Sequence:
1. Build-up starts
2. layers reduce
3. riser begins
4. crowd chant grows
5. brief silence
6. impact hits
7. full mix returns stronger
8. gameplay effect activates

Gameplay Effects:
- enemies knocked back
- tower damage boosted
- hype increases
- crowd size grows
- stage visuals intensify

==================================================
CROWD AUDIO
==================================================

Crowd audio is extremely important.

Crowd should have:
- idle murmur
- cheering
- chanting
- clapping
- panic
- gasp
- massive drop reaction

Crowd should respond to:
- hype
- panic
- enemy attacks
- boss appearance
- successful drops
- near-loss moments

The crowd is the emotional audio mirror of the game.

==================================================
ENEMY AUDIO
==================================================

Enemies should be identifiable by sound.

Mood Killer:
- low-pass dullness
- energy drain
- weak crowd response

Noise Parasite:
- static
- crackle
- glitch

Silence Drone:
- sudden muting
- dead zone sound
- missing stems

Riot Spark:
- alarm percussion
- chaotic crowd panic

Bass Eater:
- muffled bass absorption
- deep charging rumble

Boss:
The Silence Engine:
- music slowly disappears
- stems mute one by one
- emergency crowd claps become rhythm source

==================================================
MIXING RULES
==================================================

The mix must stay clear.

Rules:
- Kick and bass must not fight too much
- Crowd should not overpower music
- Corruption should be noticeable but not painful
- Drop should feel huge without clipping
- Important gameplay sounds must cut through
- Avoid too many constant high-frequency elements

Use volume automation:
- fade in layers
- fade out layers
- duck ambience during drops
- reduce music slightly for important enemy warnings

==================================================
AUDIO FILE FORMAT
==================================================

Recommended:
- OGG for browser performance and size
- MP3 as fallback only if needed

Loop files should:
- be perfectly looped
- share exact BPM
- share exact length
- start at bar 1
- have no silence at the start
- be normalized consistently

Recommended loop length:
- 4 bars for simple loops
- 8 bars for musical loops
- 16 bars for longer evolving layers

FINAL MUSIC GOAL
==================================================

The player should not feel like they are listening to background music.
