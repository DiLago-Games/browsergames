KEEP THE CROWD ALIVE - ENEMY DESIGN DOCUMENT

==================================================
CORE IDEA OF ENEMIES
==================================================

In "Keep the Crowd Alive", enemies are not just monsters walking toward a base.

They attack the festival experience.

They can threaten:
- the crowd
- the stage
- the music
- the towers
- the rhythm
- the hype meter
- the visual clarity
- the player's build strategy

The best enemy types should feel like they are ruining the show.

The player is not only defending territory.
The player is defending the vibe.

==================================================
MAIN ENEMY DESIGN PRINCIPLES
==================================================

1. EVERY ENEMY SHOULD ATTACK THE SHOW IN A UNIQUE WAY

Bad enemy design:
- fast enemy
- tank enemy
- flying enemy

Better enemy design:
- enemy that silences towers
- enemy that makes the crowd panic
- enemy that corrupts the beat
- enemy that steals hype
- enemy that creates fake applause
- enemy that forces a bad drop

2. ENEMIES SHOULD BE READABLE

The player must quickly understand:
- what the enemy does
- why it is dangerous
- how to counter it

Readable through:
- shape language
- color
- animation
- sound cue
- movement pattern
- crowd reaction

3. ENEMIES SHOULD CREATE MUSICAL PROBLEMS

Enemies can:
- desync towers
- add noise
- mute stems
- disrupt BPM
- invert resonance
- create silence zones
- distort audio feedback

4. COUNTERPLAY MUST BE CLEAR

Every dangerous enemy needs a solution.

Examples:
- Bass counters swarms
- Percussion reveals stealth
- Melody burns down priority targets
- Ambient stabilizes the crowd
- Noise counters corrupted enemies

==================================================
ENEMY ROLE CATEGORIES
==================================================

Enemies can be grouped into major roles:

1. Crowd Threats
They attack audience morale and hype.

2. Audio Threats
They attack the music system directly.

3. Tower Threats
They attack or disable player structures.

4. Pathing Threats
They change how waves move through the map.

5. Combo Breakers
They disrupt synergies and resonance chains.

6. Boss / Event Enemies
They transform the rules of the current round.

==================================================
CORE ENEMY TYPES
==================================================

==================================================
1. MOOD KILLER
==================================================

ROLE:
Crowd Threat

FANTASY:
A dead-energy enemy that drains excitement from nearby audience members.

VISUAL:
Tall, slow, grey/blue, slouched silhouette.
Could look like a faceless critic, ghost, or corporate anti-fun entity.

BEHAVIOR:
- Walks toward crowd zones
- Emits a low-energy aura
- Reduces hype in its radius
- Makes nearby crowd members stop dancing
- Can cause people to leave if ignored

GAMEPLAY EFFECT:
- Hype drain over time
- Crowd density slowly decreases
- Visual energy around the area fades

AUDIO EFFECT:
- Music becomes duller near its influence
- High frequencies fade out
- Crowd cheers get quieter

COUNTERPLAY:
- Melody towers burst it down
- Ambient towers protect crowd morale
- Bass towers push it away from audience zones

WHY IT IS GOOD:
It makes the player protect the emotional state of the crowd, not just a health bar.

==================================================
2. NOISE PARASITE
==================================================

ROLE:
Audio Threat

FANTASY:
A parasite that feeds on clean sound and turns it into distortion.

VISUAL:
Small glitchy creature with static trails.
Could flicker in and out of existence.

BEHAVIOR:
- Attaches to towers
- Corrupts their sound output
- Turns useful music layers into harmful noise
- Spreads corruption if not removed quickly

GAMEPLAY EFFECT:
- Attached tower loses resonance contribution
- Nearby towers suffer reduced synergy
- Corruption can spread across connected towers

AUDIO EFFECT:
- Static, crackle, bitcrush
- Beat becomes unstable
- Distorted tower stem leaks into the mix

COUNTERPLAY:
- Percussion towers can shake them off
- Noise towers can absorb/reverse corruption
- Manual cleanse ability could remove them

WHY IT IS GOOD:
It creates urgency without simply dealing direct damage.

==================================================
3. SILENCE DRONE
==================================================

ROLE:
Audio Threat / Zone Control

FANTASY:
A floating anti-music machine that creates pockets of silence.

VISUAL:
Smooth black drone with a pulsing mute symbol or waveform-canceling field.

BEHAVIOR:
- Floats above path or crowd
- Creates a silence field
- Towers inside the field stop producing audio
- Resonance chains passing through the field break

GAMEPLAY EFFECT:
- Music layers temporarily drop out
- Combos deactivate
- Crowd in the silence zone loses energy faster

AUDIO EFFECT:
- Sudden removal of stems
- Muffled filter effect
- Empty dead zone in the mix

COUNTERPLAY:
- Melody towers with anti-air targeting
- Bass shockwaves can destabilize the drone
- Ambient towers can keep crowd calm during silence

WHY IT IS GOOD:
It creates dramatic moments where the player literally hears the show dying.

==================================================
4. RIOT SPARK
==================================================

ROLE:
Crowd Threat / Panic Starter

FANTASY:
A chaotic enemy that triggers panic and movement disorder in the crowd.

VISUAL:
Fast, red/orange, aggressive, spark-like humanoid or creature.

BEHAVIOR:
- Rushes toward dense crowd clusters
- On contact, starts panic waves
- Panicked audience members push outward
- Can create holes in the crowd

GAMEPLAY EFFECT:
- Crowd formation breaks
- Hype drops sharply in affected area
- Some people may leave the venue
- Towers that scale with crowd density become weaker

AUDIO EFFECT:
- Crowd cheers become screams
- Beat gets unstable
- Alarm-like percussion enters briefly

COUNTERPLAY:
- Bass towers stop rushes
- Ambient towers calm panic
- Percussion towers mark and interrupt them

WHY IT IS GOOD:
It makes the crowd feel physically alive and fragile.

==================================================
5. COPYCAT
==================================================

ROLE:
Combo Breaker

FANTASY:
An enemy that mimics the player's rhythm but weaponizes it.

VISUAL:
Mirror-like or holographic enemy with waveform reflections.

BEHAVIOR:
- Scans nearby towers
- Copies their rhythm pattern
- Generates a fake counter-beat
- If left alive, it can invert resonance bonuses

GAMEPLAY EFFECT:
- Player combos become weaker
- Some resonance effects may backfire
- Copied tower type gets temporarily reduced efficiency

AUDIO EFFECT:
- Echoes of the player's own beat
- Slightly delayed mirrored rhythm
- Uncomfortable phase effect

COUNTERPLAY:
- Change tower priority
- Use Noise towers to scramble it
- Use Melody towers to kill it before it finishes copying

WHY IT IS GOOD:
It turns the player's own build into a tactical problem.

==================================================
6. FEEDBACK WRAITH
==================================================

ROLE:
Audio Threat / Punisher

FANTASY:
A ghost-like enemy that becomes stronger when the mix is too overloaded.

VISUAL:
Translucent spirit made of waveform spikes and feedback rings.

BEHAVIOR:
- Gains strength when too many towers fire in the same frequency range
- Moves faster during overcrowded audio moments
- Releases feedback bursts when damaged

GAMEPLAY EFFECT:
- Punishes stacking too many similar tower types
- Encourages varied builds
- Can temporarily stun towers with feedback bursts

AUDIO EFFECT:
- High-pitched feedback tone
- Screech pulses
- Mix becomes painful if ignored

COUNTERPLAY:
- Diversify tower frequencies
- Use Ambient towers to smooth the mix
- Use Bass towers to absorb feedback

WHY IT IS GOOD:
It prevents one-note strategies and supports the music-system fantasy.

==================================================
7. BASS EATER
==================================================

ROLE:
Specialized Tank

FANTASY:
A heavy creature that absorbs low frequencies and grows stronger.

VISUAL:
Large armored beast with pulsing subwoofer-like plates.

BEHAVIOR:
- Resists Bass towers
- Gets armor when hit by bass damage
- Stores absorbed bass energy
- Releases a shockwave when full

GAMEPLAY EFFECT:
- Counters overreliance on crowd-control towers
- Can knock back crowd members or disable nearby towers

AUDIO EFFECT:
- Bass becomes muffled when it absorbs hits
- Deep rumble grows as it charges

COUNTERPLAY:
- Melody towers pierce its armor
- Noise towers can overload its stored bass
- Percussion towers can interrupt its charge

WHY IT IS GOOD:
It forces the player to think beyond "more bass equals more control."

==================================================
8. OFFBEAT RUNNER
==================================================

ROLE:
Fast Enemy / Rhythm Disruptor

FANTASY:
A fast enemy that moves between beats and is hard to hit with synchronized attacks.

VISUAL:
Thin, sharp, jittery creature that moves in irregular bursts.

BEHAVIOR:
- Moves on offbeats
- Dodges attacks that trigger only on the main beat
- Quickly reaches crowd if ignored

GAMEPLAY EFFECT:
- Punishes builds that are too slow or too predictable
- Requires syncopated or fast towers

AUDIO EFFECT:
- Skittering clicks
- Offbeat glitch sounds
- Nervous rhythm layer

COUNTERPLAY:
- Percussion towers with fast attack patterns
- Noise towers with random timing
- Melody towers with tracking beams

WHY IT IS GOOD:
It creates a natural reason for syncopation mechanics.

==================================================
9. MONOTONY LEECH
==================================================

ROLE:
Hype Drain / Anti-Repetition

FANTASY:
A creature that feeds on repetitive loops.

VISUAL:
Slug-like audio vampire with looping waveform rings.

BEHAVIOR:
- Becomes stronger if the player keeps the same combo active too long
- Drains hype from repeated patterns
- Encourages the player to evolve the track

GAMEPLAY EFFECT:
- Reduces rewards from stale combos
- Lowers crowd excitement if not countered
- Forces build adaptation

AUDIO EFFECT:
- Loop becomes dull and flat
- Repetition gets subtly filtered
- Crowd reaction weakens

COUNTERPLAY:
- Trigger beat drops
- Switch tower modes
- Add new layers
- Use Ambient towers to refresh the crowd

WHY IT IS GOOD:
It supports the fantasy of an evolving live performance.

==================================================
10. VIP HUNTER
==================================================

ROLE:
Priority Crowd Threat

FANTASY:
An enemy that targets special high-value audience members.

VISUAL:
Predatory, spotlight-tracking enemy.
Could look like a sleek shadow assassin.

BEHAVIOR:
- Ignores normal routes when possible
- Targets VIP crowd members
- If it reaches a VIP, hype drops heavily
- May reduce end-of-round rewards

GAMEPLAY EFFECT:
- Creates priority targeting decisions
- Makes crowd composition matter
- Forces protection of special audience zones

AUDIO EFFECT:
- Sudden tension sting
- Spotlight sound cue
- Crowd gasp

COUNTERPLAY:
- Melody towers for single-target damage
- Bass towers to redirect
- Ambient shields around VIP zones

WHY IT IS GOOD:
It gives the crowd internal structure instead of being one anonymous mass.

==================================================
11. STAGE SABOTEUR
==================================================

ROLE:
Tower / Objective Threat

FANTASY:
A technician-like enemy that tries to break stage systems.

VISUAL:
Small sneaky gremlin, rogue roadie, hacker drone, or cable-cutter.

BEHAVIOR:
- Avoids combat when possible
- Moves toward power nodes, speakers, or stage modules
- Disables upgrades or lighting systems
- Can cut off stage bonuses

GAMEPLAY EFFECT:
- Stage effects temporarily disabled
- Income reduced
- Visual intensity drops
- Certain tower categories may lose buffs

AUDIO EFFECT:
- Cable pop
- Power-down hum
- Lights and stems fade

COUNTERPLAY:
- Percussion towers reveal and mark
- Melody towers eliminate quickly
- Defensive utility towers near stage modules

WHY IT IS GOOD:
It makes stage infrastructure part of the defense.

==================================================
12. CROWD SURFER
==================================================

ROLE:
Pathing Threat

FANTASY:
An enemy that uses the audience itself as a movement path.

VISUAL:
Humanoid surfing across the crowd on chaotic energy.

BEHAVIOR:
- Can bypass normal lanes once it reaches crowd edge
- Moves over audience heads
- Creates panic where it travels
- Harder to target while inside dense crowd

GAMEPLAY EFFECT:
- Breaks predictable lane defense
- Makes crowd density both a strength and risk
- Forces area-control tools

AUDIO EFFECT:
- Crowd wave sound
- Chaotic cheering turning into panic
- Doppler-like movement cue

COUNTERPLAY:
- Ambient towers calm and slow crowd movement
- Bass shockwaves knock it out of the crowd
- Percussion towers track it through dense areas

WHY IT IS GOOD:
It turns the crowd into terrain.

==================================================
13. FAKE FAN
==================================================

ROLE:
Hidden Threat / Infiltrator

FANTASY:
An enemy disguised as a normal audience member.

VISUAL:
Looks like crowd until revealed.
Subtle wrong animation, no rhythm, or flickering outline.

BEHAVIOR:
- Enters with the crowd
- Pretends to dance
- Slowly drains hype
- Can reveal itself and sabotage nearby fans

GAMEPLAY EFFECT:
- Creates paranoia
- Encourages detection mechanics
- Punishes ignoring audience health

AUDIO EFFECT:
- Slightly wrong clap
- Off-key cheer
- Hidden discord tone

COUNTERPLAY:
- Percussion scan pulse
- Ambient morale check
- Noise towers reveal anomalies

WHY IT IS GOOD:
It makes the crowd more than decoration.

==================================================
14. DROP CANCELER
==================================================

ROLE:
Event Disruptor

FANTASY:
An enemy that tries to ruin the player's biggest moment.

VISUAL:
Tall ritual-like enemy carrying a waveform-nullifying device.

BEHAVIOR:
- Appears shortly before a beat drop or ultimate activation
- If not killed, cancels or weakens the drop
- May convert stored hype into enemy shields

GAMEPLAY EFFECT:
- Creates intense countdown moments
- Makes special abilities require protection
- Adds drama before peak moments

AUDIO EFFECT:
- Rising tension
- Drop anticipation gets interrupted
- If successful, the drop collapses into silence

COUNTERPLAY:
- Focus fire
- Pre-place burst towers
- Use crowd hype shield to protect the drop

WHY IT IS GOOD:
It creates trailer-worthy clutch moments.

==================================================
15. GENRE PHANTOM
==================================================

ROLE:
Adaptive Enemy

FANTASY:
An enemy that changes resistance based on the current music style.

VISUAL:
Shapeshifting enemy made of different genre aesthetics:
neon, metal, classical, glitch, techno, etc.

BEHAVIOR:
- Reads dominant tower category
- Adapts resistance
- Forces the player to diversify music layers

GAMEPLAY EFFECT:
- Reduces damage from overused tower families
- Encourages hybrid builds
- Can change form mid-wave

AUDIO EFFECT:
- Enemy theme shifts genre
- Music briefly clashes with its form

COUNTERPLAY:
- Maintain balanced tower composition
- Use resonance combos instead of raw spam
- Use Noise towers to destabilize its adaptation

WHY IT IS GOOD:
It prevents stale meta builds.

==================================================
ADVANCED ELITE ENEMIES
==================================================

==================================================
16. THE CRITIC
==================================================

ROLE:
Elite Crowd Threat

FANTASY:
A powerful enemy that judges the show and spreads negativity.

BEHAVIOR:
- Stands at range
- Sends negative review waves into the crowd
- Reduces hype gain from nearby towers
- Makes audience members less responsive

SPECIAL MECHANIC:
If the player activates a strong resonance near The Critic, it becomes stunned by the quality of the performance.

COUNTERPLAY:
- Create a high-quality combo near it
- Burst it during stun window

WHY IT IS GOOD:
It turns "good music" into a direct weapon.

==================================================
17. THE BOOTLEGGER
==================================================

ROLE:
Economy Threat

FANTASY:
An enemy that steals revenue, merch sales, or crowd rewards.

BEHAVIOR:
- Avoids main defenses
- Moves toward income structures
- Reduces post-round rewards
- May sell fake tickets, causing unstable crowd members to enter

COUNTERPLAY:
- Detection
- Fast single-target towers
- Crowd security upgrades

WHY IT IS GOOD:
It attacks long-term progression instead of immediate survival.

==================================================
18. THE BPM BREAKER
==================================================

ROLE:
System Threat

FANTASY:
An enemy that attacks the global tempo of the show.

BEHAVIOR:
- Emits tempo instability waves
- Towers may temporarily fire late or early
- Beat-synced abilities become less reliable

AUDIO EFFECT:
- Warped tempo
- Tape-stop effects
- Drifting beat grid

COUNTERPLAY:
- Stabilizer towers
- Ambient anchors
- Destroy before full tempo disruption

WHY IT IS GOOD:
It attacks the deepest system of the game.

==================================================
19. THE LIGHT EATER
==================================================

ROLE:
Visual / Morale Threat

FANTASY:
An enemy that consumes stage lights and makes the festival feel dead.

BEHAVIOR:
- Darkens map areas
- Reduces visibility
- Crowd dances less in darkness
- Hidden enemies become harder to detect

COUNTERPLAY:
- Re-light zones
- Melody beams
- Stage upgrade defenses

WHY IT IS GOOD:
It connects visuals directly to gameplay mood.

==================================================
20. THE HYPE THIEF
==================================================

ROLE:
Resource Threat

FANTASY:
An enemy that steals accumulated hype and stores it.

BEHAVIOR:
- Drains hype on contact with crowd
- Carries stolen hype as a glowing core
- If killed quickly, stolen hype is recovered
- If it escapes, hype is permanently lost

COUNTERPLAY:
- Priority targeting
- Burst damage
- Crowd barrier abilities

WHY IT IS GOOD:
It creates immediate risk/reward tension.

==================================================
BOSS DESIGN
==================================================

Bosses should not only have more health.
They should change the rules of the show.

==================================================
BOSS 1: THE SILENCE ENGINE
==================================================

FANTASY:
A massive machine built to shut down all music.

PHASE 1:
- Sends Silence Drones
- Creates small mute zones

PHASE 2:
- Expands silence fields
- Removes one music stem at a time

PHASE 3:
- Attempts a full-stage shutdown
- Player must keep enough rhythm nodes active

CORE EXPERIENCE:
The player hears their music slowly disappear and fights to bring it back.

COUNTERPLAY:
- Protect rhythm nodes
- Destroy silence generators
- Use crowd claps as emergency rhythm source

==================================================
BOSS 2: THE RIOT CONDUCTOR
==================================================

FANTASY:
A boss that conducts panic like an orchestra.

PHASE 1:
- Sends Riot Sparks into the crowd

PHASE 2:
- Creates crowd wave attacks
- Turns dense crowd zones into danger zones

PHASE 3:
- Attempts to split the entire crowd apart

CORE EXPERIENCE:
The crowd becomes the battlefield.

COUNTERPLAY:
- Calm zones
- Bass barriers
- Stage-wide morale drop

==================================================
BOSS 3: THE FALSE DJ
==================================================

FANTASY:
A rival performer who copies and corrupts the player's music.

PHASE 1:
- Copies tower loops

PHASE 2:
- Creates fake resonance chains

PHASE 3:
- Starts a musical duel
- Player must outperform it with stronger combos

CORE EXPERIENCE:
Your own strategy is mirrored against you.

COUNTERPLAY:
- Change rhythm composition
- Break fake towers
- Build higher-quality resonance

==================================================
BOSS 4: THE FEEDBACK GOD
==================================================

FANTASY:
A gigantic entity born from overloaded sound.

PHASE 1:
- Punishes frequency stacking

PHASE 2:
- Sends Feedback Wraiths

PHASE 3:
- Creates dangerous sound overload zones

CORE EXPERIENCE:
The player must balance power and clarity.

COUNTERPLAY:
- Diversify sound layers
- Use smoothing towers
- Avoid overstacking one damage type

==================================================
BOSS 5: THE NIGHT ENDER
==================================================

FANTASY:
The final boss that tries to end the festival before sunrise.

PHASE 1:
- Attacks stage systems

PHASE 2:
- Sends all enemy categories in mixed patterns

PHASE 3:
- Begins countdown to total crowd collapse

FINAL MOMENT:
The player must trigger one final massive drop to revive the crowd and end the night.

CORE EXPERIENCE:
The ultimate "Keep the Crowd Alive" moment.

ENEMY WAVE STRUCTURE
==================================================

EARLY WAVES:
Introduce simple crowd threats.
Examples:
- Mood Killer
- Offbeat Runner
- Noise Parasite

MID WAVES:
Introduce disruption and mixed threats.
Examples:
- Silence Drone
- Riot Spark
- Bass Eater
- Stage Saboteur

LATE WAVES:
Introduce enemies that attack systems.
Examples:
- BPM Breaker
- Genre Phantom
- Drop Canceler
- Hype Thief

BOSS WAVES:
Change rules dramatically and test the player's understanding.

==================================================
ENEMY COUNTER MATRIX
==================================================

BASS TOWERS:
Strong against:
- Riot Spark
- Crowd Surfer
- Offbeat Runner
- swarms

Weak against:
- Bass Eater
- Feedback Wraith

PERCUSSION TOWERS:
Strong against:
- Noise Parasite
- Fake Fan
- Offbeat Runner
- Stage Saboteur

Weak against:
- heavily armored enemies

MELODY TOWERS:
Strong against:
- Mood Killer
- VIP Hunter
- Silence Drone
- Drop Canceler

Weak against:
- swarms
- adaptive enemies

AMBIENT TOWERS:
Strong against:
- panic
- hype drain
- silence damage
- crowd collapse

Weak against:
- direct enemy rushes

NOISE TOWERS:
Strong against:
- Copycat
- Genre Phantom
- Noise Parasite
- Bass Eater overload

Weak against:
- crowd morale if overused

==================================================
CROWD INTERACTION IDEAS
==================================================

Enemies can affect the crowd in different ways:

1. PANIC
Crowd members run away from danger.

2. APATHY
Crowd stops dancing and hype gain falls.

3. CORRUPTION
Crowd claps offbeat and disrupts resonance.

4. EXODUS
Crowd members leave the venue.

5. MOSH PIT CHAOS
Crowd density becomes unstable.
Can be good or bad depending on player tools.

6. VIP LOSS
Special audience members leave, reducing rewards.

==================================================
AUDIO FEEDBACK PER ENEMY
==================================================

Each enemy should have a clear sound identity.

Examples:
- Mood Killer: dull low-pass filter
- Noise Parasite: crackle/static
- Silence Drone: sudden stem removal
- Riot Spark: alarm percussion
- Copycat: delayed echo
- Bass Eater: muffled sub rumble
- Offbeat Runner: skittering clicks
- Drop Canceler: failed riser
- Hype Thief: reversed crowd cheer
- BPM Breaker: warped tempo

This lets skilled players identify threats by ear.

==================================================
VISUAL READABILITY
==================================================

Enemy silhouettes should communicate function:

- Round/small = swarm or parasite
- Tall/thin = morale threat
- Floating = audio-zone threat
- Heavy/wide = tank
- Glitching = corruption
- Mirror-like = copying/adapting
- Bright red/orange = panic/rush
- Black/void-like = silence

==================================================
SPAWN DESIGN
==================================================

Enemies should not always enter from normal lanes.

Possible spawn methods:
- backstage entrances
- crowd infiltration
- falling from drones
- appearing through speaker feedback
- emerging from corrupted light zones
- entering as fake fans
- spawning during beat drops

This keeps waves surprising and thematic.

==================================================
DESIGN WARNING
==================================================

Do not overload early gameplay.

The first playable prototype should use only 5 enemy types:

1. Mood Killer
2. Noise Parasite
3. Silence Drone
4. Riot Spark
5. Bass Eater

These five already test:
- crowd morale
- audio corruption
- silence zones
- panic
- tower composition

More enemies can come later.

==================================================
CORE ENEMY DESIGN SUMMARY
==================================================

Enemies in "Keep the Crowd Alive" should be designed around one question:

"How does this enemy ruin the show?"

If the answer is interesting, visual, audible, and counterable,
then the enemy belongs in the game.

The strongest enemies are not the ones with the most health.
They are the ones that make the player feel the festival slipping away.
