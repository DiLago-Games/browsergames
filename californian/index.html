How to play

  • Move your van across five lanes with the keyboard, by tapping the road, or with the on-screen buttons.
  • Collect glowing records for big points and a growing streak bonus.
  • Avoid cars, cones, and roadblocks. Every hit costs one life and resets your streak.
  • Survive long enough to push the level higher and squeeze out a bigger score.

Road signs

Records add points and extend your hot streak.

Scoring

Every second on the road adds cruise points. Records are worth more as your streak grows, so clean lines and calm lane changes beat panic swerves.
